## 2024-06-25 - Custom Range Slider Focus States & Live Regions
**Learning:** When styling custom range sliders (input type="range") with WebKit pseudos, applying `:focus-visible` to the input itself doesn't show focus on the thumb by default. We must target `.slider:focus-visible::-webkit-slider-thumb` to provide visual feedback for keyboard users without breaking mouse interaction. Additionally, dynamically updated UI elements, such as the real-time `#alert-list` in `web/index.html`, require `aria-live='assertive'` and `role='log'` to ensure screen readers announce new items.
**Action:** Always ensure that custom styled forms (like sliders) receive a dedicated `:focus-visible` pseudo-class targeted correctly at their inner pseudo-elements to maintain keyboard accessibility. Always attach `aria-live` regions to dynamic, real-time alert logs.
